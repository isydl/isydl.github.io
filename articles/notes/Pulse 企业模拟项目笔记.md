# Company Terms

## Inc.

### English

Incorporated

### Chinese Meaning

股份有限公司 / 注册成立的公司

### Japanese

株式会社

### Explanation

Inc. is a legal company suffix used mainly
in the United States.

It indicates that the company is officially
incorporated as a legal entity.

### Examples

Apple Inc.

Microsoft Inc.

### Notes

Inc. is not the same as "Company".

Company:
A general word meaning "公司".

Inc.:
A legal company form.

---

## Ltd.

### English

Limited

### Chinese Meaning

有限公司

### Japanese

有限会社 / 株式会社

### Explanation

Ltd. indicates a company with limited liability.

### Example

ABC Ltd.

### Notes

Different countries use different legal forms.

# Business Terms

## Enterprise

### English

Enterprise

### Chinese Meaning

企业级 / 大型企业

### Japanese

企業向け

### Explanation

In IT, Enterprise usually refers to
large organizations or business customers.

### Examples

Enterprise Customer

企业客户

企業向け顧客

Enterprise System

企业级系统

企業向けシステム

### Notes

Enterprise does not mean "company".

Company = 公司

Enterprise = 企业规模 / 商业组织

---

# Project Management Terms

## Owner

### English

Owner

### Chinese Meaning

负责人 / 所有者

### Japanese

責任者

### Explanation

In project management,
Owner means the person responsible
for a specific area.

### Examples

Project Owner

项目负责人

プロジェクト責任者

Technical Owner

技术负责人

技術責任者

Owner does not mean the company owner.

# Project Document Terms

## Project Charter

### English

Project Charter

### Chinese Meaning

项目章程 / 项目启动书

### Japanese

プロジェクト憲章

### Explanation

A Project Charter officially starts
a project.

It defines:

- Project purpose
- Business goal
- Owner
- Stakeholders
- Timeline

### Notes

Project Charter answers:

"Why are we doing this project?"

Not:

"How do we build it?"

# Translation Notes

## 项目

### English

Project

### Japanese

プロジェクト

### Alternative Japanese

案件（あんけん）

### Difference

プロジェクト:

A planned project with goals and timeline.

案件:

A business case or customer engagement.

Example:

Customer Project

顧客案件
