# Pulse Technologies Inc.

A company that provides an enterprise-grade SaaS e-commerce platform.

Core business area:

- Enterprise E-commerce Platform.
- Order Management System.
- Inventory Management System.
- Payment Integration.
- Customer RelationShip Management(CRM)

---

## Company Discovery

The company identified the following business situation:

- The number of customers in the Japanese market has been continuously increasing.

Customers requested:

> Develop an enterprise e-commerce platform capable of supporting large-scale promotional campaigns.
