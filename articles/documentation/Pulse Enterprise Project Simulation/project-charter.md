## Project Goal

Build an enterprise e-commerce platform
for customers in the Japanese market.

The platform should support:

- Large-scale promotional campaigns
- Order management
- Inventory management
- Payment integration
- Customer management

## Business Background

The company has experienced rapid customer growth
in the Japanese market.

To support increasing customer demand,
the company will develop a new enterprise e-commerce platform.
